# Accident History Guidance

## Purpose

Accident records provide evidence about reported collisions, damage, or
vehicle repairs. Accident evidence should be interpreted together with its
severity, description, source, and related vehicle records.

## Accident Severity

Accident records may indicate different levels of reported severity:

- minor
- moderate
- major
- unknown

Severity describes the available record. It does not by itself establish the
extent of mechanical, structural, or safety-related damage.

## Major Accident Evidence

A major accident record is a significant signal that requires additional
review.

Relevant verification evidence may include:

- repair invoices
- insurance claim records
- inspection reports
- service-center records
- photographs of damage and repairs
- manufacturer repair records
- structural inspection results

The available accident record alone may not establish the current condition
of the vehicle.

## Conflicting Accident Information

If accident records from different sources conflict, the conflict should be
preserved as an evidence issue.

The system should identify:

1. what each source reports,
2. whether the sources can be reconciled,
3. which source has stronger provenance,
4. what additional evidence is required.

A conflict should not automatically be treated as proof that one source is
false.

## Verification Actions

When a significant accident is reported, review:

- the original accident or claim record,
- repair documentation,
- inspection results,
- service history before and after the event,
- available photographs,
- subsequent vehicle condition evidence.

The purpose is to determine what is established by the available records and
what remains uncertain.

## AI Grounding Rule

The AI must distinguish between:

1. a reported accident,
2. reported accident severity,
3. reported damage or repairs,
4. and a confirmed current vehicle condition.

The AI must not claim that a vehicle has structural damage, mechanical damage,
or a safety defect unless the supplied evidence explicitly establishes that
finding.

A major accident record should be described as evidence requiring further
review when the available records do not establish the resulting vehicle
condition.

## Vehicle-Specific Interpretation

Vehicle-specific accident dates, severity, descriptions, and sources must come
from the vehicle evidence supplied to the intelligence system.

This document provides general accident interpretation guidance only. It must
not be used as a source of vehicle-specific accident facts.
